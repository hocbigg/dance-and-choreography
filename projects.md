## Choreograph your own solo dance piece

- [10 Simple Steps for Starting Choreography (Dance Masterclass)](https://www.dance-masterclass.com/blog/10-simple-steps-for-starting-choreography) - A step-by-step creative walkthrough for generating movement motifs, setting choreographic constraints, and structuring a solo dance piece.

## Transcribe your own movement score

- [Labanotation Fundamentals (Dance Notation Bureau)](https://www.dancenotation.org/labanotation-fundamentals) - An interactive methodology guide teaching how to transcribe human movement into written scores using the vertical Labanotation staff.

## Produce your own dance film

- [Making Video Dance: A Step-by-Step Guide to Creating Dance for the Screen (Katrina McPherson/Routledge)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781138699137) - [Paid Book] The standard production manual covering storyboard drafting, camera-dancer framing, site shooting, and editing rhythms for screen-based choreography.

## Plan your own rehearsal schedule

- [How to Schedule Dance Rehearsals That Your Team Actually Shows Up To (Coryo)](https://coryo.app/blog/how-to-schedule-dance-rehearsals) - A structured production workflow for collecting performer availability, establishing rehearsal milestones, and backward-planning toward a live performance date.
- [Preparing Your Dancers for a Performance: 5 Strategies (Studio Pro)](https://gostudiopro.com/blog/preparing-your-dancers-for-a-performance-5-strategies/) - A practical preparation guide covering run-through scheduling, physical recovery, and pre-performance operational checklists.

## Write your own dance review

- [Guidelines for Writing a Dance Review (Dawn Loring and Julie Pentz/Human Kinetics)](https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/guidelines-for-writing-a-dance-review) - A four-part analytical methodology dividing a dance review into objective description, movement analysis, contextual interpretation, and critical evaluation.
- [Stage to Page: A Guide to Dance Reviewing and Writing (Sanjoy Roy)](https://sanjoyroy.net/2015/12/stage-to-page-a-guide-to-dance-reviewing-and-writing/) - A working dance critic's practical guide to active viewing, sensory note-taking, and drafting evocative movement prose.

## Design your own dance lesson plan

- [Street Dance Curriculum Guide (Mika Lemoine and Rashidi Omari/Destiny Arts Center)](https://destinyarts.org/street-dance-curriculum/) - An open pedagogical guide providing lesson-scaffolding templates, youth development practices, and structured drills for technique, composition, and freestyle.
- [Creative Dance for All Ages (Anne Green Gilbert/Human Kinetics)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781492500506) - [Paid Book] The foundational dance pedagogy text detailing the five-part lesson planning framework: warm-up, concept exploration, skill development, creative study, and reflection.

## Draft your own artist grant proposal

- [Grant Writing Basics: How To Put Your Best Foot Forward with Funders (Tracie Holder/New York Foundation for the Arts)](https://www.nyfa.org/blog/grant-writing-basics-how-to-put-your-best-foot-forward-with-funders/) - A practical guide to defining choreographic vision, establishing project urgency, meeting funding guidelines, and writing competitive project narratives.
- [Artist Grant Proposal Writing Handbook (First Peoples' Cultural Council)](https://fpcc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/grant-writing-handbook.pdf) - A step-by-step handbook covering core grant components, including artist statements, work sample curation, and balanced project budgets.

## Develop your own dance studio business plan

- [Dance Studio Business Plan: 7 Steps to Get Started (Studio Pro)](https://gostudiopro.com/blog/dance-studio-business-plan) - A comprehensive operational guide providing a step-by-step roadmap for market research, service pricing, facility budgeting, and studio financial planning.

## Build your own dance preservation archive

- [Documenting Dance: A Guide for Dance Preservation (Libby Smigel/Dance Heritage Coalition)](https://danceusa.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/page_uploads/DocumentingDance.pdf) - A 65-page preservation handbook establishing standards for archiving rehearsal processes, choreographic notes, multi-camera recordings, and post-performance reception materials.

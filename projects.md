# Projects: Dance and Choreography

This page organizes practical, project-based guides by the concrete artifact each project produces. Learners choose the deliverable they want to create — a choreographed dance, a filmed performance, a written critique, a taught class, or a funded project — and discover the underlying frameworks as they build the artifact.

## Choreograph an Original Dance Piece

- [10 Simple Steps for Starting Choreography (Dance Masterclass)](https://www.dance-masterclass.com/blog/10-simple-steps-for-starting-choreography) - a step-by-step creative process guide covering how to collect ideas, use improvisation, set creative constraints, record yourself, and structure movement into a finished piece.
- [Labanotation Fundamentals (Dance Notation Bureau)](https://www.dancenotation.org/labanotation-fundamentals) - a free, interactive introduction to the standard system for recording choreography in written notation, used to preserve and restage dance works.
- [Documenting Dance (Dance Heritage Coalition)](https://danceusa.s3.us-east-2.amazonaws.com/page_uploads/DocumentingDance.pdf) - a 65-page practical guide to documenting dance works across seven contexts, with case studies showing how to document effectively even with limited resources.

## Produce and Rehearse a Live Performance

- [How to Schedule Dance Rehearsals That Your Team Actually Shows Up To (Coryo)](https://coryo.app/blog/how-to-schedule-dance-rehearsals) - a practical workflow for planning rehearsals, collecting availability, working backward from a performance date, and setting measurable goals for each session.
- [Preparing Your Dancers for a Performance: 5 Strategies (Studio Pro)](https://www.gostudiopro.com/blog/preparing-your-dancers-for-a-performance-5-strategies/) - a preparation guide covering rehearsal scheduling, rest and nutrition, stage-fright tools, pre-performance checklists, and team motivation.

## Write a Dance Critique or Review

- [Guidelines for Writing a Dance Review (Human Kinetics / Dawn Loring and Julie Pentz)](https://us.humankinetics.com/blogs/excerpt/guidelines-for-writing-a-dance-review) - a structured framework that breaks a written review into four sections: description, analysis, interpretation, and evaluation.
- [Stage to Page: A Guide to Dance Reviewing and Writing (Sanjoy Roy)](https://sanjoyroy.net/2015/12/stage-to-page-a-guide-to-dance-reviewing-and-writing) - a working critic's guide to the craft of describing, interpreting, and evaluating dance, with concrete advice on choosing strong verbs and building imagery.

## Produce a Dance Film or Video Work

- [Making Video Dance: A Step-by-Step Guide to Creating Dance for the Screen (Katrina McPherson)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9780415774862) - the definitive workbook on dance-film production, following the entire process from conception through shooting and editing, with exercises, a production diary, and practitioner interviews. This is a paid book; no adequate free equivalent covers the full production process at this depth.

## Teach a Dance Class or Workshop

- [Dance Teachers: Here's How to Build an Effective Lesson Plan (DanceTeacherWeb)](https://www.danceteacherweb.com) - search 'How to Build an Effective Lesson Plan' on DanceTeacherWeb for a breakdown of each component of a dance-specific lesson plan and how to use it across styles and levels.
- [The Primary Teacher's Guide to Teaching Dance (PE Planning)](https://peplanning.org.uk) - search 'The Primary Teacher's Guide to Teaching Dance' on PE Planning for a beginner-focused guide to structuring dance lessons for students with no prior dance training.
- [Dance Curriculum Tips, Lesson Activities, and Template (Sawyer)](https://www.hisawyer.com) - search 'Dance curriculum tips, lesson activities, and template' on Sawyer for an editable lesson plan template and a step-by-step approach to writing a dance curriculum.

## Fund and Organize a Dance Project

- [The Field Guide To Grant Applications (The Field)](https://www.thefield.org/resource-field-guide-to-grant-applications) - a detailed guide to the common elements of grant proposals for artists, including letters of inquiry, cover letters, project descriptions, and budgets.
- [Dance Studio Business Plan: 7 Steps to Get Started (Studio Pro)](https://gostudiopro.com/blog/dance-studio-business-plan) - a seven-step guide to developing a comprehensive dance studio business plan, including a template and example.
- [CDC Program Evaluation Framework, 2024 (Centers for Disease Control and Prevention)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11444645/) - a widely used six-step framework for planning and conducting evaluations of community programs, applicable to community dance programs and outreach initiatives.

## Curation Summary

**Choreograph an Original Dance Piece** — included because creating original choreography is the central creative act of the discipline, and notation and documentation are the standard methods for preserving what you create. All three resources are free.

**Produce and Rehearse a Live Performance** — included because staging work for an audience is the natural endpoint of choreographic practice, and both guides are free, practical, and focused on the logistical realities of rehearsal and performance.

**Write a Dance Critique or Review** — included because critical writing is a distinct skill that deepens analytical understanding of dance while producing a publishable artifact. Both guides are free.

**Produce a Dance Film or Video Work** — included because screendance is now a major professional outlet and creative medium in its own right. This category includes one paid book because no free resource covers the full dance-film production process with comparable depth; I checked for free courses and guides on dance-film production and found none adequate.

**Teach a Dance Class or Workshop** — included because teaching is both a common career path and one of the most effective ways to deepen your own technical understanding. These resources are listed by title and platform rather than direct URL because I could not verify their exact addresses; all three are freely accessible once located on their respective platforms.

**Fund and Organize a Dance Project** — included because securing funding and running sustainable programs are practical necessities for independent dance makers. The resources mix free guides (grant writing, business planning) with a widely applicable free evaluation framework from the CDC.

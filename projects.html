Choreograph your own solo dance piece

  • 10 Simple Steps for Starting Choreography (Dance Masterclass) - A step-by-step creative walkthrough for generating movement motifs, setting choreographic constraints, and structuring a solo dance piece.

Transcribe your own movement score

  • Labanotation Fundamentals (Dance Notation Bureau) - An interactive methodology guide teaching how to transcribe human movement into written scores using the vertical Labanotation staff.

Produce your own dance film

  • Making Video Dance: A Step-by-Step Guide to Creating Dance for the Screen (Katrina McPherson/Routledge) - [Paid Book] The standard production manual covering storyboard drafting, camera-dancer framing, site shooting, and editing rhythms for screen-based choreography.

Plan your own rehearsal schedule

  • How to Schedule Dance Rehearsals That Your Team Actually Shows Up To (Coryo) - A structured production workflow for collecting performer availability, establishing rehearsal milestones, and backward-planning toward a live performance date.
  • Preparing Your Dancers for a Performance: 5 Strategies (Studio Pro) - A practical preparation guide covering run-through scheduling, physical recovery, and pre-performance operational checklists.

Write your own dance review

  • Guidelines for Writing a Dance Review (Dawn Loring and Julie Pentz/Human Kinetics) - A four-part analytical methodology dividing a dance review into objective description, movement analysis, contextual interpretation, and critical evaluation.
  • Stage to Page: A Guide to Dance Reviewing and Writing (Sanjoy Roy) - A working dance critic's practical guide to active viewing, sensory note-taking, and drafting evocative movement prose.

Design your own dance lesson plan

  • Street Dance Curriculum Guide (Mika Lemoine and Rashidi Omari/Destiny Arts Center) - An open pedagogical guide providing lesson-scaffolding templates, youth development practices, and structured drills for technique, composition, and freestyle.
  • Creative Dance for All Ages (Anne Green Gilbert/Human Kinetics) - [Paid Book] The foundational dance pedagogy text detailing the five-part lesson planning framework: warm-up, concept exploration, skill development, creative study, and reflection.

Draft your own artist grant proposal

  • Grant Writing Basics: How To Put Your Best Foot Forward with Funders (Tracie Holder/New York Foundation for the Arts) - A practical guide to defining choreographic vision, establishing project urgency, meeting funding guidelines, and writing competitive project narratives.
  • Artist Grant Proposal Writing Handbook (First Peoples' Cultural Council) - A step-by-step handbook covering core grant components, including artist statements, work sample curation, and balanced project budgets.

Develop your own dance studio business plan

  • Dance Studio Business Plan: 7 Steps to Get Started (Studio Pro) - A comprehensive operational guide providing a step-by-step roadmap for market research, service pricing, facility budgeting, and studio financial planning.

Build your own dance preservation archive

  • Documenting Dance: A Guide for Dance Preservation (Libby Smigel/Dance Heritage Coalition) - A 65-page preservation handbook establishing standards for archiving rehearsal processes, choreographic notes, multi-camera recordings, and post-performance reception materials.
